# Functional Operations

## Initial administration sequence

1. Create academic cycle.
2. Upload Faculty Master.
3. Upload Student Master.
4. assign multiple supervisors to semester-section combinations.
5. map students to supervisors.
6. open team formation.
7. supervisors create teams and generate Team IDs.
8. faculty publish projects.
9. teams apply to one project at a time.
10. project owners discuss, accept or reject.
11. accepted allocations become public.

## Role responsibilities

- **Admin:** masters, users, settings, audit and corrections.
- **Coordinator:** academic-cycle monitoring and exception management.
- **Supervisor:** create teams from assigned students and monitor them.
- **Faculty:** publish project ideas and decide applications to own projects.
- **Student:** use the automatically associated team identity to apply.

## Public information

Project title/write-up, technology stack, status, team identity, student names, project owner, supervisor and accepted allocation may be public. Phone, email, internal remarks, credentials and private documents remain protected.

## Data correction

Do not delete accepted allocation records directly. Use a documented administrative reversal workflow and preserve an audit entry.
